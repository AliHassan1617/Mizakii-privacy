Privacy Policy

FitCheck AI  •  Effective date: July 3, 2026  •  Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FitCheck AI ("the App", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. FitCheck AI lets you submit photos of your outfit to receive an AI-generated style rating ("roast"), keep a history of past results, and optionally share results with other users.

By using FitCheck AI, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photos You Submit

When you use the "Rate My Fit" feature, the App accesses your device's camera and/or photo library (with your permission) to capture or select a photo of your outfit. This photo is sent to our AI processing service to generate a style score, written "roast," and related feedback.

1.2 Account & Profile Information

If the App offers account creation or a public leaderboard, we may collect a username, avatar, and any captions or comments you choose to add to a roast. This information may be visible to other users if you opt into public or shared features (e.g. the recent-roasts feed, leaderboard).

1.3 Roast History

Past roasts (photo, score, and written feedback) are stored so you can revisit them in the App's History screen. This data is tied to your device or account and is not sold to third parties.

1.4 Device & Usage Data

We may automatically collect limited technical information necessary to operate and maintain the App, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash/error logs. As of this version, FitCheck AI does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly before that update is released.

In short: the main thing we handle is the photo you submit for a roast, plus whatever username or caption you attach to it. We don't collect contacts, precise location, or payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your AI-powered outfit score and written roast.
  • To display your roast history back to you within the App.
  • To operate features like the recent-roasts feed and leaderboard, if you choose to participate.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App, including diagnosing crashes and bugs.
  • To communicate with you about the App, if you contact us or opt into notifications.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Service Providers

Photos submitted for a roast are processed using third-party AI/image-analysis service providers in order to generate your score and feedback. These providers process the image solely to return a result to the App and are contractually or technically restricted from using your photo for their own purposes beyond providing this service. We do not sell your photos.

The App also uses Google Fonts to load typefaces, which may involve a network request to Google's font-delivery servers.

4. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your roast may be visible to other users only if you participate in shared/public features of the App (for example, a public feed, leaderboard, or the "Share the Roast" action). We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users, or in connection with a business transfer (e.g. merger or acquisition), subject to this policy continuing to apply to your information.

5. Data Retention

Roast history is retained until you delete it from the App or delete your account. If you uninstall the App without deleting your account/data first, associated server-side data (if any) may persist until you request deletion as described below.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can delete individual roasts from your History at any time within the App, where available.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).
  • You can revoke camera/photo permissions at any time via your device's system settings; doing so will limit the App's core functionality.
  • Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

FitCheck AI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed or stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
