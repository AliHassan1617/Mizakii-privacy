Mizakii · Gold Rush

Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 5, 2026
Android application: com.mizakii.goldrush

This policy explains how Gold Rush, developed by Ali Hassan under Mizakii (also spelled Mizaki), handles information in the current prototype and internal-testing version.

In brief: Gold Rush stores your game progress and preferences on your device. It does not require an online account and does not send your player name or gameplay saves to a Mizakii server. The current version has no advertisements, analytics service, in-app purchases, or automatic crash-reporting service.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores the following information so you can continue playing:

  • An optional player name or nickname displayed in your village. You may use a made-up name or continue as “Captain”; a real name is not required.
  • Game progress, including gold and gems, building and troop levels, upgrades and their timers, your battle deck, training results, and cleared obstacles.
  • Audio preferences, tutorial completion, daily-gift status, and saved village or interface layouts.

On Android, these saves use app-local storage and Android preferences. In the browser version, they use the browser’s local storage. The game uses them to display your village, restore progress, calculate upgrades and rewards, and remember your choices. These records are not automatically uploaded to the developer.

2. Network access, permissions, and third-party services

The Android app has an internet permission, but its gameplay and artwork are packaged with the app. The current game does not use an online gameplay service, multiplayer server, advertising network, tracking identifiers, or analytics SDK. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or photo library.

If you open this privacy-policy page or another external link, your browser connects to that website. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting service may receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser information, under GitHub’s privacy statement. This page itself contains no advertising or analytics scripts.

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, store, diagnostics, or backup information under your settings and Google’s privacy policy. Those services are separate from Gold Rush’s own gameplay data handling.

3. Android backup and restore

The app permits Android’s system backup. Depending on your device, operating system, and account settings, Android may back up app-local data and restore it on another installation. Those backups are managed by your device or backup provider, not by a Mizakii game server. You can manage backup settings through your device or provider.

4. Information you choose to send us

If you email us for help or send feedback, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you provide. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the game. Please do not send passwords, signing keys, or unnecessary sensitive information. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the request and related issues; you can contact us to request deletion, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. The current game does not transmit saved player profiles or gameplay progress to advertisers or analytics providers. Email and hosting providers process information needed to deliver their respective services.

5. Retention and deletion

Your local game data remains on the device until it is cleared or the app is removed. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Gold Rush → Storage → Clear storage/data to remove the local save. In the browser, clear the game site’s stored data. This resets progress and cannot be undone within the game.

Removing local data does not necessarily remove an operating-system backup. Manage or delete backups separately using your device or backup-provider settings. We cannot remotely access or delete a save that exists only on your device.

6. Children and security

The current version does not request a date of birth or require a real name. Its device-local approach applies to all players. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy concerns or information voluntarily sent to support.

App-local data is protected by the normal controls of your device and operating system. Keep your device secure and avoid including sensitive information in a player nickname. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

If future versions introduce accounts, online features, ads, analytics, or payments, we will update this policy to describe their data handling before introducing those features, and provide any required notices and consent options. The date above identifies the latest revision.
