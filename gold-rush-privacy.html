MIZAKII STUDIOS · GOLD RUSH

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026
Android app: com.mizakii.goldrush

This policy describes Gold Rush, developed by Ali Hassan under Mizakii Studios (also called Mizakii or Mizaki), including its online account and payment features. Older offline prototype versions did not use all of these features.

Gold Rush saves online, supports optional Google sign-in, and processes gameplay and purchase information needed to run the game. It does not contain an advertising or third-party analytics SDK. You can request deletion without reinstalling the game, or delete your account from supported versions of the app.

1. Information the game handles

  • Accounts and sign-in: a generated account ID and device ID, device-authentication credentials, session credentials and an optional Google account identifier. Device credentials and server sessions use security protections; the server stores hashes of device secrets and session tokens. Google sign-in tokens are verified, not stored as a copy of your Google password. We do not ask for or store your Google password, or maintain a directory of Google email addresses.
  • Player information: your chosen captain and village names. You may use a nickname rather than a real name. Names may be visible to opponents.
  • Progress and activity: villages, balances, buildings, cards, decks, upgrades, rewards, tutorial state, achievements, pass progress, battle results and replays. Account creation, sign-in, last-seen times and app build numbers are recorded to operate and support the service.
  • Purchases: product and purchase identifiers, verification tokens while a purchase or refund needs processing, entitlement and delivery status, purchase times, whether a receipt is a test receipt, and refund/reconciliation records. Google Play processes your payment method; Gold Rush does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Network and service information: Cloudflare processes requests and connection metadata such as IP addresses. The game uses short-lived hashed-IP and account-based rate-limit records to limit abuse. Operational logs are intended to avoid passwords, raw purchase tokens, Google sign-in tokens and complete save contents.
  • Device preferences: local audio, interface, update-prompt and optional reminder settings, cached saves and unsent save requests. Notification reminders are optional and scheduled on your device.
  • Support: your email address, messages and attachments if you contact us.

2. How we use this information

We use it to authenticate players, save and restore villages, match and run battles, deliver rewards, verify purchases, reconcile refunds, provide account deletion and support, troubleshoot failures, and reduce fraud and abuse. The private player dashboard is restricted to the developer. We do not sell your personal information or use gameplay profiles for targeted advertising.

3. Service providers and sharing

Our game backend uses Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects and D1. Google provides Android, Google Play distribution and billing, and optional Google sign-in. GitHub Pages hosts this policy. These providers may process information needed to provide and secure their services, including outside your country. Opponents receive the public information needed to play and view their own battle records, not your authentication secrets or purchase tokens.

See Cloudflare's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Permissions, local storage and recovery

The app uses internet access. If you choose local reminders, Android may ask for notification permission; boot-related permission lets opted-in reminders resume after a restart. The game does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or photo library.

Android credentials are encrypted using a device-bound Android Keystore key. Supported online builds exclude account credentials and cached game data from Android cloud backup and device transfer; older prototype versions permitted system backups. Google-linked villages can be restored by signing in again. A guest account may be unrecoverable after losing its device credentials. Clearing app storage or uninstalling is not the same as deleting the server account.

5. Delete your account and associated data

Open the Gold Rush account-deletion page.

In a supported app version, open Settings → Delete account, read the warning and type DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Google-linked players must verify the same Google account again. Guest players confirm from the device that owns the account. Finish active battles and resolve unfinished purchase deliveries or refunds first. Deleting an account removes both villages, progression, currencies, cards, decks, your own battle history, profile names, Google link and device sign-in credentials from the live account. It does not delete your Google account or automatically refund purchases.

Without the app, email a deletion request to alihassan.ah1617@gmail.com. Include your account ID if available, captain and village names, and write from your linked Google email address if applicable. We will verify ownership. Do not send passwords, one-time sign-in codes, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information. Contact the same address if you cannot complete deletion in the app.

6. Retention and limited exceptions

Active account data is retained to operate your game until you request deletion. Migration and recovery snapshots are restricted to the developer and are included in account-data deletion. Deletion confirmation records are kept for up to 30 days to recover from a lost network reply. Accounts without retained transaction references can then be physically removed.

Minimal pseudonymous payment, refund and receipt-reuse records may remain as needed for reconciliation, fraud prevention and applicable financial obligations. They do not retain your Google sign-in identity or village progression. Shared battle records already held by another player may remain in that player's history. Temporary battle-server state expires within 24 hours.

Cloudflare disaster-recovery history currently expires after up to seven days on our Free plan. We must reapply completed deletions before serving players after any backup restoration. Older Android/provider backups are controlled separately by your device or backup provider. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle requests and related issues; contact us to request deletion, subject to applicable obligations.

7. Children and security

The game does not ask for a date of birth or require a real name. Do not include personal or sensitive information in public nicknames. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy concerns. Store audience declarations and any required parental-consent measures must match the version and audience offered.

We use HTTPS, authenticated accounts, access restrictions and purchase verification. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and Google account secure.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when data handling changes and provide any required notices or choices. The date above identifies this revision.
