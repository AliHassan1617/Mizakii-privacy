Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Banknote Identifier ("the App", "we", "our") is a banknote scanning, identification and collection app developed by Mizakii. This Privacy Policy explains what permissions and data the App uses, how they are handled, and your rights as a user.

1. Permissions We Request

Camera

The App requires access to your device camera to allow you to photograph banknotes for identification. Images captured through the scanner are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to any external server in the current version of the App.

Internet Access

The App requests internet access to load reference images for banknote catalogue entries displayed within the App. In future versions, internet access will also be used to support user account creation, cloud synchronisation of your collection, and database lookups for banknote identification. No personal data is transmitted over the internet in the current version.

2. Data We Currently Collect

The current version of the App operates in local-only mode. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers at this time. All collection data is stored on your device only.

3. Future Data Collection

Future versions of the App will introduce optional user accounts and cloud sync features. When these features are introduced, the following data may be collected:

  • Name and email address (for account creation)
  • Your scanned banknote collection (for cloud backup and sync across devices)
  • Device identifiers (for account security)

This Privacy Policy will be updated before any such features are released, and you will be notified in-app. All future data collection will be opt-in where possible.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not access your contacts, microphone, or any other device sensors.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Security

Because all data currently remains on your device, it is protected by your device's own security mechanisms. Future cloud features will use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and industry-standard security practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will update this Privacy Policy as new features are added. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice and by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
