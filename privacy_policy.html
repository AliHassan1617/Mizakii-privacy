Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Numismatist: Heritage Vault ("the App", "we", "our") is a banknote identification and collection app developed for collectors of historical currency. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Information We Collect

The App currently operates in a local-only mode. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

  • Camera: The App requests access to your device camera solely to allow you to photograph banknotes for identification purposes. Images captured through the scanner are processed on-device and are not uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.
  • Collection data: Notes saved to your collection are stored locally on your device only.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics services.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Camera Permission

Camera access is required for the core functionality of the App — scanning and identifying banknotes. The App will prompt you for permission before accessing the camera. You may deny this permission; however, the scanning feature will not be available. You can revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings.

4. Internet Access

The App requests internet permission solely to load preview images for banknote catalogue entries that are displayed within the App. No personal data is transmitted over the internet.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Security

Because all data remains on your device and no personal information is transmitted to external servers, your data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

7. Changes to This Policy

As the App evolves (for example, when account features or cloud sync are introduced), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. We will notify users of material changes through an in-app notice or by updating the "Last updated" date above.
